## Item 1 — the Schody (A_R4) residual (priority)

Published 3.64 m². Measured **3.412 m² at the finish face (−6.3%)**, **3.601 m² at
structure (−1.1%)**. This is the project's largest residual.

The record currently claims a "~0.25 m² landing nub" at the stairwell's north-east corner
explains it. **That claim is now doubtful and you should treat it as unproven.** The
coordinator cropped the region and found:

- The tread-free landing at the top of the flight is `x 4517..5427, y 7599..8550` — that is
  *already inside* the modelled polygon `x 4500..5450, y 4760..8550`. It is not extra area.
- The alleged nub is a thin-lined box at roughly `x 5427..5740, y 7615..8540` (≈313 × 925 mm)
  with a **rounded corner** and a **filled dot** at its north-east. It appears in the same
  position relative to the stair on `plan_ground.png` too.
- Thin lines + rounded corner + a filled dot read much more like a **balustrade post and
  handrail return**, or furniture, than like floor slab.

Adding it to the spec to make a number fit would be inventing geometry. Rule 3 and rule 4
in `TASKS.md` forbid it.

**A competing explanation fits better and is what you should test first.** Schody is the
only room bounded by *guards* rather than plastered masonry, so the 20 mm/face finish
convention may not apply to those edges at all:

| Reading of the south edge | Area | vs 3.64 |
|---|---|---|
| north face of guard A_W7 (y 4760) — current spec | 3.6005 | −1.1% |
| guard centreline (y 4730) | 3.629 | −0.3% |
| south face of guard (y 4700) | 3.6575 | +0.5% |

The guard is 60 mm and sits at `y 4700..4760`; Antresola's floor edge at `y 4700` is
**printed** (425 cm from the south interior face) and is not in question. The open question
is only whether the guard stands *on* the Antresola slab, *on* the void edge, or centred.

Determine which, from the drawing. Then say plainly whether the nub is floor, and if it is
not, say what it is. **"Unidentified" is an acceptable and useful answer** — better than a
confident wrong one. If you conclude the nub is not floor, that is a finding, and the
README entry and the `A_R4` / `A_W8` notes are wrong and must be rewritten.

## Item 2 — roof window plan depth (README Outstanding #2)

`1600 × cos 35° = 1310.6 mm` against a traced plan depth of **1271 mm** — 3.1% apart.
Either the on-slope height is nearer 1552 mm, or the traced projection is short.

The roof windows should be visible in elevation. Measure the visible sash height against a

## Item 3 — gable windows not centred (README Outstanding #3)

`A_O1`/`A_O2` sit at `offset` 4275 on 8550-long gable walls; centred would be 3775, so the
spec has them **500 mm off-centre**, with each window's jamb landing exactly on the ridge
line. That is a suspicious coincidence — it is equally consistent with a tracing error.

`elevation_side_1.png` and `elevation_side_2.png` are the gable elevations. A gable window
either is or is not centred under the ridge apex, and that is directly visible. Settle it.
Note the two chimneys are also visible on these elevations and give you a cross-check on
the frame.

## Method notes

- Crop and upscale generously; `Image.LANCZOS` for reading linework, `NEAREST` when you
  need to count individual pixels.
- Quote pixel coordinates *and* the mm they convert to, with the scale you used, every
  time. A bare millimetre figure cannot be checked by the next reader.
- State a ± on every traced measurement. At 40.89 px/m one pixel is 24.5 mm; a 3.1%
  disagreement on 1271 mm is 39 mm, i.e. **1.6 px** — so item 2 may simply not be
  resolvable at this raster resolution. If so, say that; it is the correct answer.
