# T09 — Test 3: global invariants

**Depends on:** T07 (kernel), T11 (3D generator, for volume/roof) · **Parallel with:** T08, T10

## Goal

`tests/test_invariants.py` — five scalars that constrain the model from directions the
room areas cannot reach: vertical geometry, exterior envelope, and roof.

## The five invariants

| Invariant | Target | Tolerance | Validates |
|---|---|---|---|
| Usable area | 163.57 m² | ±1% | Interior layout + wall thicknesses |
| Footprint (pow. zabudowy) | 154.42 m² | ±1% | Exterior envelope at ground level |
| Cubature | 849.27 m³ | ±1.5% | Storey heights + roof volume |
| Roof area | 216.8 m² | **±6%** (see below) | 35° pitch + eave overhangs |
| Building height | 7.09 m | ±30 mm | Section geometry, ground → ridge |

Roof pitch is asserted **exactly** at 35.0° — it is a spec input, confirmed by T17, not a
derived value. Building-height tolerance is 30 mm rather than 10 mm because
`roof_buildup_vertical` carries a measured ±30 mm uncertainty; the reconstruction lands
9 mm out.

## The critical rule about ridge height

**Ridge height must fall out of the geometry, never be forced into it.**

Build the roof generatively: from the eaves line, the 35° pitch, and the wall-plate
elevation, compute where the ridge lands. *Then* compare that computed value to 7.09 m.

If you instead drag or set the ridge to 7.09 m by construction, the assertion becomes a
tautology and you have destroyed the only independent check on the roof geometry. This is
the single easiest way to silently invalidate the project's central claim.

Same logic for roof area: 216.8 m² at a known 35° pitch and known footprint pins the
**eave overhang**, which is otherwise unpublished. Solve for it, record it as
`derived: true`, and let the assertion confirm it.

## Roof: resolved, with one caveat

T17 resolved the roof (`docs/roof-resolution.md`). **No `xfail` markers needed** — build
the ridge from the chain in `spec/meta.json` and it lands 9 mm from the printed +6.77 and
9 mm from the published 7.09 m. Both are genuine checks, not tautologies, because
`roof_buildup_vertical` is the only derived input in that chain.

**The one exception: roof area.** Measured overhangs (600 mm eaves, 590 mm verge) give
**227.9 m²** against the published **216.8 m²** — a 5% gap. 216.8 implies a 0.44 m uniform
overhang, contradicting 0.60 m measured on two independent images.

Assert roof area as a **±6% sanity band**, not a ±1% hard check, and document why in the
docstring. This is a deliberate, evidence-based loosening of a single invariant whose
published definition we do not know — not a tolerance widened to make a test pass. Do not
loosen it further, and do not "fix" it by shrinking the overhang to 0.44 m: T17 explicitly
declined that, because the overhang is measured and the roof-area convention is not known.

Note this does not weaken the pitch conclusion at all — 40.7° would require a 0.197 m
overhang against 0.60 m measured.

## Definitions to pin down before asserting

These invariants are only meaningful if you match Archon's definitions. Determine and
document each:

- **Pow. zabudowy (footprint)** — measured to the outer face of the exterior wall
  *including* finish, at ground level. Excludes eaves, terraces, and steps. Confirm
  whether the boiler room / any annexe is inside or outside this boundary.
- **Cubature** — gross volume, exterior faces, from foundation top to roof surface.
  Whether the plinth is included is the usual ambiguity; treat plinth height as a
  free variable solved against this figure.
- **Roof area** — the sloped surface area of both planes including overhangs, not the
  horizontal projection. At 35° the ratio to projected area is `1/cos(35°) ≈ 1.2208`;
  use that as a sanity check on your own computation.

## Failure diagnosis

Cubature and building height are coupled through storey heights, and both are partly
`derived`. If cubature fails but every area passes, the error is almost certainly
**vertical** — storey height or plinth offset — not planar. Do not re-examine the floor
plan; look at the section.

If roof area fails while height passes, the eave overhang is wrong.

## Acceptance criteria

- All five assertions implemented with the tolerances above
- Ridge height demonstrably computed, not assigned — include a test asserting that
  changing `pitch_deg` changes the computed ridge height
- Each invariant's measurement definition documented in a docstring
- Failure output names which invariants failed and by how much, together
