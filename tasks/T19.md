## Item 1 — Schody (`A_R4`): a convention error, not missing geometry

T18's finding, and it reverses the existing record: the published 3.64 m² is the **drawn
slab opening measured at structure with no finish allowance on any edge**. The L-shaped
polygon actually drawn is 3.645 m² (+0.1%). The 20 mm/face allowance is *excluded*, not
mis-tuned — the L has a 10.084 m perimeter, so staying inside ±1% needs ≤ 4.1 mm/face.

Why the exemption is principled rather than convenient: under PN-ISO 9836 a stair is
counted as the plan projection of flight and landings — the slab opening — and two of
`A_R4`'s four edges are a void edge and a guard, which have no plaster to deduct.
`A_SO2`'s existing note already says the opening is 3.6005 m² and "matches Schody's raw
structure-convention area"; that is the same fact from the other side.

**Implement the recommended minimal form**, not the L:

- `A_R4` polygon `x 4500..5450, y 4700..8550` → **3.6575 m², +0.5%**.
- `A_R4` is measured at **structure**, exempt from `finish_allowance`.

Two things to get right:

1. **The south edge.** The printed value is 4700 (chain `A_C5`: 450 wall + 4250 printed
   Antresola depth). The guard `A_W7` currently occupies 4700..4760, so today that 60 mm
   band belongs to neither room. **Antresola's own floor must keep its printed 450..4700
   depth** — it currently reconciles and must not regress. So the band goes to `A_R4`,
   i.e. the guard stands on the void side of the slab edge, which is what a balustrade at
   a stairwell head actually does. Do not move `A_W7`'s centreline to 4670: that eats
   30 mm off Antresola across its full 6.96 m width, ≈0.21 m², and breaks a passing check
   to fix a different one.
2. **The exemption must be data, not a special case in the kernel.** Add an optional
   per-room field (e.g. `measure_to: "structure"`) to `spec/schema.json`, set it on `A_R4`
   only, and have the geometry kernel honour it. A hardcoded `if room_id == "A_R4"` is not
   acceptable — the carve-out has to be visible in the spec and justified in the room's
   `note`, because it is the only room in the project that does not take the allowance.

**Do NOT add a 0.25 m² "nub" to the existing polygon.** T18 measured that at +6.8% — it is
the one change that is definitely wrong. The nub *is* floor (floor tone 236 not void tone
197, and the stair's walking line originates inside it), but it is only admissible together
with narrowing the flight from 950 to 872 mm, and those two corrections cancel. That
cancellation is why the wrong rectangle scores −1.1%.

## Item 2 — roof-window plan depth: no discrepancy, a one-pixel error

The recorded 1271 mm was measured to the *inner* faces of two dashed lines rather than
their centres — exactly one pixel low. Line-centre to line-centre the depth is
**1296 ± 12 mm** against `1600·cos 35° = 1310.6`, a 0.6 px residual. The method is
calibrated in place: the same box measured the same way in x gives 781.7 mm against a
printed 780 callout, 0.07 px.

- `spec/meta.json`, the identical note on all three roof windows: extent is
  `y = 1149..2446`, depth **1296 ± 12**, residual 14.6 mm / 1.1%. Drop the "either the
  on-slope height is nearer 1552 mm or the traced projection is short" sentence — the
  projection *was* short, by one pixel, and that is now measured.
- Same figures repeated in `src/kotewki/generator.py` (~line 185) and
  `src/kotewki/report.py` (~line 866).
- `tests/test_generator.py::test_roof_window_plan_depth_matches_its_on_slope_height`:
  docstring says 3.1%, now 1.1%. **Its companion assertion that the identity does not hold
  at 1% must be deleted, not re-tuned** — at 1.1% it is marginal, and once the depth is
  corrected it fails for the right reason. Deleting an assertion that has stopped being
  true is correct; loosening it to keep it green is not.

Changing the bounds changes geometry, so expect `test_export.py`'s recorded mesh figures
and `test_invariants.py`'s `MEASURED` to shift. Update recorded values to what is measured;
do not widen a tolerance.

and a centred window is excluded by 28 px, on two independent renders. Add the
corroboration to the `A_O1`/`A_O2` notes — near jamb on the ridge, centred excluded by

Record separately, without changing anything: the renders put the glazing at

below the eaves reading as a concrete spandrel. The `100/273` callout is the authority and
a marketing render is not — this is a note for the next reader, not a defect.
