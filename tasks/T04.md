# T04 — Transcribe ground floor

**Depends on:** T02 (schema), T03 (images) · **Parallel with:** T05

## Goal

Convert `data/source/plan_ground.png` into **`spec/ground.json`**.

You own that file exclusively. Do not write to `spec/meta.json` (owned by T02) or
`spec/attic.json` (owned by T05, running concurrently). Prefix every id with `G_`
(`G_W1`, `G_R1`, `G_O1`) — ids must be globally unique across the merged spec.

This is the **only** task in the project where a human/agent reads pixels. Everything
downstream is mechanical. Accuracy here bounds the accuracy of the whole model.

## Method — in this order

### 1. Dimension chains first, geometry second

Before placing a single wall, transcribe **every printed dimension chain** into
`dimension_chains[]`. Each chain is a run of segment dimensions plus a printed overall.
Record them exactly as printed, in millimetres, in order along the axis.

Then run T06's chain-closure check. **Do not proceed to wall geometry until every chain
sums.** A chain that does not close means a misread digit, and finding it now costs
minutes; finding it after building geometry costs hours.

### 2. Wall centrelines

Place walls as centreline segments in a single level-local coordinate system, origin at
the building's outer south-west corner, +x east, +y north. Derive coordinates
**arithmetically from the chains**, not by eyeballing positions against the image.

Exterior wall thickness: Porotherm 250 + EPS 200 + plaster. Total ≈ 465–480 mm.

Printed chains are to **raw structure**; published areas are *w świetle ścian* (finished
faces). T03's evidence indicates a **20 mm per-face finish allowance** reconciles the two
to within 0.05%. Use 20 mm, mark it `derived: true`, and let T15 confirm it across all 14
rooms. Do not silently bake it into the transcribed dimensions — it belongs to the
geometry kernel's `measure_to` parameter, not to the chain values. Interior partitions on Polish plans of this type are typically
Porotherm 115 or 250 — read which from the plan hatching and dimension chains, do not
assume.

### 3. Rooms

One `rooms[]` entry per room in the published ground-floor table — **all 14**:

Wiatrołap 5.35 · Hol 1.81 · Pokój 11.99 · Łazienka 3.48 · Pokój 11.99 ·
Salon z jadalnią 30.57 · Hol 2.30 · Pokój 13.05 · Garderoba 3.96 · Pralnia 4.94 ·
Kotłownia 7.31 · Łazienka 5.85 · Spiżarnia 1.54 · Kuchnia 12.04

Carry each room's `published_area` verbatim. Note the two `Pokój 11.99` rooms are
genuinely identical in area — do not assume that is a transcription duplicate.

### 4. Openings

Every window and door: `offset` along host wall, `width`, `height`, `sill`, `kind`,
`swing`. Door swing direction matters for layout fidelity and is readable from the arc
on the plan. Where the plan gives a stolarka schedule, prefer it over scaling.

If sill heights are not printed, mark them `derived: true` with a stated assumption
(typically 850–900 mm for windows, 0 for doors and balcony doors).

## Hard rules

- **The plans print CENTIMETRES; the spec stores millimetres.** Transcribe
  `dimension_chains` verbatim in cm into the `_cm` fields and do no arithmetic — the
  loader multiplies by 10. Everywhere else, millimetres. A missed ×10 yields a house one
  tenth the size that still closes every chain, so read the "Units" section of `README.md`
  before you start.
- Expected overall building size: **17.10 × 9.00 m** (T03 confirmed from two independent
  chains plus the site plan). If your transcription does not reproduce that, stop.
- Known-good chains to check yourself against: bottom `470 · 224 · 1016 = 1710`,
  left `820 · 80 = 900`.
- **Integer values only.**
- Every value not printed on the plan gets `derived: true`. Be strict about this. The
  distinction between "Archon printed this" and "I inferred this" is the project's main
  defence against confident wrongness.
- Where a dimension is illegible, do **not** guess silently — mark it `derived: true`,
  record the inference basis in a `note`, and list it in your final report.

## Acceptance criteria

- All ground-floor chains close exactly (T06 passes)
- 14 rooms present, each with `published_area`
- Wall network is closed (T10 will verify)
- Report lists every `derived: true` value and its justification

## Report back

- Count of transcribed vs derived values
- Any illegible dimensions and how they were resolved
- Overall building dimensions as they fall out of the chains (these are **not** the
  25.1 × 17.0 m plot figure — that is the minimum plot size, not the building)
