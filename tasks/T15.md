# T15 — Confirm the finish allowance

**Depends on:** T04 (transcribed ground floor), T07 (kernel), T08's dual-convention runner

> **Scope changed after T03.** The original task was "determine which measurement norm
> Archon used". That is now answered: the page states **PN-ISO 9836** explicitly, four
> times, and areas are measured *w świetle ścian* — to finished faces, plaster included.
> No investigation needed. What remains is the quantitative follow-on.

## The remaining question

Printed chains are to **raw structure**. Published areas are to **finished faces**. The
gap is a per-face finish allowance, and its value is not published.

T03's evidence points to **20 mm per face**:

| Room | Chain | Raw | +20 mm/face | Published | Error |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pokój | 380 × 323 cm | 12.274 m² | 11.994 m² | 11.99 m² | +0.04% |
| Łazienka | 260 × 140 cm | 3.640 m² | 3.482 m² | 3.48 m² | +0.05% |

Two samples, both landing inside 0.05%. Compelling — but two rooms is not fourteen, and
both are simple rectangles. Confirm or refute it across the full set.

## Method

1. Run T08 with `finish_allowance` swept over 0 / 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 mm per face
2. Report aggregate error across all 14 ground-floor rooms at each value
3. Pick the minimum; confirm it is a clear minimum, not a flat basin

## Interpreting the result

- **20 mm confirms across all 14 rooms** → set the constant, mark it `derived: true` with
  this evidence, done.
- **A different value wins cleanly** → equally fine. Record it and the evidence. Do not
  privilege 20 mm because it appeared first; two rectangular rooms are a weak prior.
- **No value fits all rooms** → the residual is not a uniform finish allowance. Most
  likely cause: **non-rectangular rooms**. A room with a niche, chimney, or notch has a
  published area that a single width × height product cannot reproduce at any allowance.
  Identify which rooms fail, check their plan geometry for notches, and report them
  individually rather than distorting the global constant to average them out.
- **The basin is flat** (10–25 mm all within tolerance) → say so, pick 20 mm, and state
  that room areas are only verified to the precision the flatness permits.

The last two outcomes are legitimate results. **Do not manufacture false confidence** by
reporting a winner from a 0.1% difference across a 1% tolerance.

## Deliverables

- `measurement_norm` set to `"PN-ISO 9836"` in `data/published.json` (T03 left it null

- `docs/norm-resolution.md` — the sweep table, per-room errors at the chosen value, and
  any rooms that resist explanation
- Explicit statement of residual uncertainty and what it means for T08's assertions
